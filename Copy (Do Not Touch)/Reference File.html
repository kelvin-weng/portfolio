About

Finding structure in data, and in the frame.

I work in data governance and architecture across financial services, currently at Macquarie Group, where I help translate messy, enterprise-wide data problems into models, standards, and tooling that people can actually use. I care about the layer underneath the dashboard — the taxonomy, the metamodel, the rule that makes the number trustworthy in the first place.

Before Macquarie, I spent time at Westpac building and testing data quality rules across large financial datasets — the kind of work that rewards patience and a good eye for what's actually going wrong.

Photography is the other half of how I pay attention. Where the day job is about defining structure, a camera is about noticing it — light, geometry, a moment that won't repeat. I shoot mostly around Sydney; more of it lives on Instagram while I build out a proper gallery here.
